# Ecology Energy Flow Notes

## Purpose

These notes explain how energy moves through an ecosystem and how matter is recycled. Energy flows in one direction through a food web, while matter can be used again by living and nonliving parts of the ecosystem.

## Producers Capture Energy

Producers are organisms that make their own food. Most producers, such as green plants and algae, use sunlight to convert water and carbon dioxide into sugar during photosynthesis. The sugar stores chemical energy that can later be used by the producer or eaten by another organism.

## Consumers Obtain Energy

Consumers cannot make their own food. Herbivores eat producers, carnivores eat other consumers, and omnivores eat both plants and animals. A food chain shows one possible path of energy transfer. A food web connects many food chains and shows that most organisms have more than one food source.

## Energy Decreases Between Trophic Levels

A trophic level is a feeding position in a food chain or food web. When one organism eats another, only part of the energy in the food becomes new biomass. Organisms use much of the energy for movement, growth, repair, and maintaining body temperature. The remaining energy is released as heat. For this reason, less usable energy is available at higher trophic levels.

## Decomposers Recycle Matter

Decomposers, including many fungi and bacteria, break down dead organisms and wastes. They return nutrients to soil, water, and air. Producers can then take in some of those nutrients again. Decomposers do not create new energy; they help recycle matter.

## Matter Cycles

Carbon, water, nitrogen, and other materials move through living things and the environment. For example, plants take in carbon dioxide during photosynthesis. Animals obtain carbon by eating plants or other animals. Respiration and decomposition return carbon dioxide to the environment.

## Example Food Web

In a pond ecosystem, algae are producers. Snails and tadpoles may eat algae. Small fish may eat tadpoles, and herons may eat small fish. If algae decrease sharply, organisms that depend on algae may have less food, and the change can affect several levels of the food web.

## Key Ideas to Check

Energy flows from the sun to producers and then through consumers. Matter is reused through cycles and decomposition. A food web is more realistic than a single food chain because it shows multiple feeding relationships.
